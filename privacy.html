Speedster: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Speedster is an offline arcade racing game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

What the game saves

To remember your progress, the game saves a few small values only on your own device, in the browser/app storage (localStorage):

  • your coins, the cars you own and their upgrades;
  • your best distance on each Free Ride map, the Time Trial levels you cleared and your best times;
  • whether sound is muted and whether you have seen the "how to play" hint.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android: Settings > Apps > Speedster > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the game.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no online leaderboards.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party code.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No network requests other than loading the game's own files. Once installed, it plays fully offline.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds something online), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
